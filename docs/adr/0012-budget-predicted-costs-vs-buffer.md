# Monthly budget splits into predicted costs and buffer

The monthly budget splits into "predicted costs" (fixed monthly expenses only) and "buffer" ((quarterly expenses / 3) + (yearly expenses / 12)); the two are summed only for the actual account transfer. Predicted costs is the figure to compare actual spending against; irregular (quarterly/yearly) costs are smoothed into a buffer that tops up the account every month regardless of whether a bill is actually due that month, so one isn't mistaken for the other. Supersedes the prior single blended "set-aside" figure, which still exists as the sum for the transfer amount.
